About Money Manager

A calm, private way to keep track of your money, without handing it to anyone else.

Why it exists

Most finance apps want an account, an internet connection and access to your data. Money Manager takes the opposite approach: everything stays on your phone, and you decide when to make a backup and where to keep it. It is free to use, with no ads and no subscription.

Who makes it

Money Manager is designed and built by shohansk, an independent developer.

How it is built

The app is built with React and TypeScript and packaged for Android with Capacitor. Your records are kept in an SQLite database on the device, and the app makes no network requests.

Contact

Questions, bug reports and suggestions are welcome on GitHub Issues. Please don't send real financial data or backup files.
