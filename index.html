Free, offline, private

Your money, on your phone, and nowhere else.

Money Manager is a personal finance app for Android. Track daily spending, budgets, investments, loans and savings goals in your own currency. There is no account to create and nothing leaves your device.

Get it on Google Play

Coming soon to Google Play.

Everything in one place

Simple enough for daily expenses, detailed enough to tell you whether your investments are actually making money.

Income, expenses and transfers

Record what comes in and goes out, with categories, subcategories, notes and tags. Move money between accounts.

Accounts

Cash, bank, mobile wallet and credit card. Balances are always calculated from your entries, so they never drift.

Monthly budgets

Set a limit per category. Subcategories count toward it, and you get a warning at 80% and again at 100%.

Investments with real profit

Stocks, funds, deposits, gold, crypto, property or a small business. Buys, sells, dividends, fees and valuations give you profit and ROI.

Loans

Money you lent and money you borrowed, with repayments, outstanding balance and overdue flags.

Savings goals

Set a target and a date, add contributions and see how close you are.

Recurring items

Salary, rent, subscriptions and instalments are added when due, and missed ones catch up the next time you open the app.

Dashboard and reports

Net worth, monthly totals, savings rate, spending by category and cash-flow trends for any date range.

Backup you control

Export a backup file or a CSV of transactions whenever you like, and restore it on any phone.

Private by design

Money Manager works entirely offline. Your financial records are stored in a database on your phone and are never sent anywhere.

Read the privacy policy

  • No account, no sign-in, no server
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking
  • The app makes no network requests
  • Optional 4 to 6 digit PIN lock
  • Free, with no in-app purchases or subscription

One thing to know. Because there is no server copy, uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes everything. Export a backup regularly and keep it somewhere safe. How to back up.
