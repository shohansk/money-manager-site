Log spending in a few taps

Tap the big plus button from anywhere, enter the amount, pick a category, done. Everything lands in a clean list grouped by day.

Set a monthly limit for each category. The bar turns amber at 80% and red when you go over.

  • Sub-category spending counts toward the parent
  • Copy last month’s budgets in one tap

Private by design

Your finances are nobody else’s business

Money Manager has no servers to send your data to. Your records live in a database on your phone, and you decide when to make a backup and where to keep it.
